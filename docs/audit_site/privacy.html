Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-25 · Contact:

1. Data we collect

  • TikTok (and other platform) account data from Login Kit: open_id, username, display name, avatar; access and refresh tokens.
  • Content the user submits: video files, captions, hashtags, scheduling data.
  • Technical logs: request statuses and errors (tokens are never logged in clear text).

2. Why we collect it

To authenticate the user, publish and schedule the user’s own content, show publication status, and diagnose errors.

3. Storage

Tokens are stored on our server with restricted access. Video files are temporary and deleted after publication or on request. We do not sell personal data.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens remain until the user disconnects the account. Users may revoke access at any time and request full deletion via .

5. Security

Server access is restricted; secrets are not exposed in public repositories or client apps.

6. Children

The App is not intended for users under 13.
